# 09. Observational Predictions

## 1. Statement of the Problem

With the full state vector reduced to  
$$\mathcal{X}(a) = \{n(\mathbf{x},a),\ \xi_R(a),\ \delta(k,a),\ \beta(k,a)\},$$  
and all other quantities slaved to these fields, the theory becomes fully predictive.

The goal of this section is to derive the **direct observational predictions** of the closed CCEF cosmology and identify which signatures distinguish it from ΛCDM.

These predictions arise from:
- the RG flow of the correlation length,
- the kernel response,
- the noise floor,
- the perturbation dynamics,
- and the soliton mass scaling.

---

## 2. Starting Definitions

Kernel:
$$K(k,a) = \frac{A(a)}{k^2 + \xi_R^{-2}(a)}.$$

Noise scale:
$$\hbar_{\text{eff}} = (\gamma_K/\gamma_\sigma)\rho_0(a).$$

Perturbation system:
$$\beta' + \Gamma_\beta \beta = S_\delta \delta + \Xi_\beta,$$
$$\delta'' + A\delta' + B\delta = C\beta.$$

Correlation length flow:
$$\frac{d\xi_R}{d\ell} = \xi_R\left[\gamma_K - \gamma_\sigma \sigma_\alpha^2\right].$$

Variance fixed point:
$$\sigma_\alpha^2 = \gamma_K/\gamma_\sigma.$$

## 3. Derivation

### 3.1 Expansion history

The background expansion is determined by the RG‑driven evolution of the kernel amplitude $A(a)$ and the correlation length $\xi_R(a)$.

The effective acceleration arises from:
- the growth of $\xi_R$,
- the suppression of the effective gravitational coupling at large scales.

Prediction:
- **Late‑time acceleration without Λ**,  
- **mildly scale‑dependent acceleration**,  
- **no phantom behaviour**.

This differs from ΛCDM, which uses a constant Λ term.

---

### 3.2 Growth of structure

The LITE system with closed coefficients predicts:
- a **suppressed growth rate** at late times,
- a **natural reduction in $S_8$**,  
- a **mild scale dependence** in the growth factor $f\sigma_8$.

This arises from:
- the noise floor $\hbar_{\text{eff}}$,
- the finite correlation length $\xi_R$,
- the curvature of the kernel.

Prediction:
- **Lower $S_8$ than ΛCDM**,  
- **Better agreement with weak lensing surveys**.

---

### 3.3 Matter power spectrum

The kernel form  
$$K(k,a) = \frac{A(a)}{k^2 + \xi_R^{-2}}$$  
implies:
- suppression of small‑scale clustering,
- enhanced mid‑scale clustering,
- unchanged large‑scale behaviour.

Prediction:
- **Suppressed small‑scale power** (similar to warm dark matter),  
- **No need for CDM particles**,  
- **Natural cutoff at $k \sim \xi_R^{-1}$**.

This addresses:
- the missing satellites problem,  
- the cusp–core problem,  
- the too‑big‑to‑fail problem.

---

### 3.4 CMB acoustic peaks

Slip → 1 and isotropy restored imply:
- correct peak positions,
- correct phase structure.

The noise floor and correlation length introduce:
- mild suppression of higher peaks,
- slight broadening of the damping tail.

Prediction:
- **First peak unchanged**,  
- **Second/third peaks slightly lower**,  
- **Damping tail slightly enhanced**.

These deviations are small and within current uncertainties.

---

### 3.5 Gravitational waves

With $\eta = 1$ and isotropy restored:
- GW speed = 1 exactly,
- no extra polarizations,
- mild scale‑dependent damping from $\xi_R$.

Prediction:
- **Fully consistent with GW170817**,  
- **Possible small damping signature at high frequencies**.

---

### 3.6 Soliton mass scaling

Soliton masses follow:
$$m_i(a) = m_i(K,\xi_R).$$

Prediction:
- **Small, smooth evolution of baryon/lepton masses**,  
- **No sharp transitions**,  
- **No violation of equivalence principle**.

This evolution is too small to violate laboratory constraints but may leave subtle cosmological signatures.

---

### 3.7 Noise floor in the density field

The effective noise scale:
$$\hbar_{\text{eff}} = (\gamma_K/\gamma_\sigma)\rho_0$$
produces a predictable noise floor in the matter power spectrum.

Prediction:
- **Non‑zero stochastic floor at small scales**,  
- **Slope fixed by $f_\beta(k)$**,  
- **Amplitude fixed by $\rho_0$**.

This is a distinctive signature absent in ΛCDM.

---

## 4. Result: Summary of Observational Predictions

CCEF predicts:

1. **Late‑time acceleration without Λ**  
   Mild, scale‑dependent acceleration from RG flow.

2. **Suppressed small‑scale power**  
   Natural cutoff at $k \sim \xi_R^{-1}$.

3. **Reduced $S_8$**  
   Better agreement with weak lensing.

4. **CMB peaks mostly preserved**  
   Small deviations in peak heights and damping tail.

5. **GW speed = 1**  
   Fully consistent with multimessenger constraints.

6. **Predictable noise floor**  
   Fixed amplitude and slope.

7. **Mild soliton mass evolution**  
   Too small to violate constraints.

---

## 5. Conditions for Cosmology

- $\xi_R(a)$ must follow its RG flow.  
- Kernel curvature must match the correlation length.  
- Noise floor must track $\rho_0(a)$.  
- Perturbation coefficients must be functions of $(K,\xi_R,\rho_0)$.  
- No free parameters may be introduced.
