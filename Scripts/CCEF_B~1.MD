# Can CCEF host a boost-Goldstone photon? — the escape route is closed by A3

The proposed clean resolution: photon = Goldstone of spontaneously-broken Lorentz boosts
(non-linear realization), exactly relativistic, while matter keeps A3. Checked against
CCEF's structure. **Result: the mechanism is structurally unavailable — the same A3 term
it was meant to evade explicitly breaks the symmetry it would need.**

## The fatal obstruction (definitive)
A Goldstone exists only for a symmetry that is **exact in the action** and *spontaneously*
(not explicitly) broken. So the prerequisite is: **are Lorentz boosts a symmetry of the
CCEF action?**

CCEF's kinetic structure is Zt|∂_t n|² + A1|∇n|² + (A3/2)(∇²n)²:
- time: 2nd order; space: 4th order ⇒ z=2 **anisotropic (Lifshitz)**.
- The A3 term is (spatial Laplacian)² — manifestly **not** boost-invariant. (A boost-
  invariant 4-derivative term would be (□n)² = ((∂_t²−∇²)n)².)

⇒ **Boosts are EXPLICITLY broken by A3.** They are not a symmetry of the fundamental
action. There is therefore **no exact boost symmetry to spontaneously break, and no
protected boost-Goldstone.** A would-be boost-Goldstone is a *pseudo*-Goldstone, corrected
precisely by the explicit-breaking term A3 — so it inherits the A3 k⁴. The escape is closed
by A3 itself.

The dispersion makes it concrete: w_CCEF² = A1k² + A3k⁴ vs the Lorentz value A1k². The
"protection" the Goldstone was supposed to give (forcing exact z=1) is exactly what A3
prevents — LV grows as (k/k_UV)² and reaches order-1 at k_UV.

## Why each requirement fails
1. **Massless?** A boost-Goldstone would be massless by Goldstone's theorem *only if boosts
   were exact*. They aren't (A3 breaks them), so masslessness isn't protected either.
2. **Protected from A3?** No — protection would require the non-linear Lorentz Ward
   identity, which requires exact Lorentz, which A3 forbids. The mode inherits A3.
3. **Couples to matter/charge?** Moot, since (1) and (2) already fail; and CCEF's actual
   vector (CP¹ a_μ) is a massive n-bilinear, not a boost-Goldstone.

## The two remaining logically-consistent options (neither is the clean win)
- **(i) Tiny A3 (~1e-15, Planckian k_UV):** Lorentz-safe for all sectors including a future
  photon. But the soliton-scale-matched B2 then ≈0.097 ⇒ baryon E·R≈31 (~7× proton). To
  reproduce the proton at this A3, the a₂ prefactor would have to be **~8× below** the
  derived value — well beyond plausible O(1) slack.
- **(ii) Replace the Lifshitz regulator (∇²n)² with a Lorentz-invariant (□n)²:** gives exact
  Lorentz invariance and no LV at any k — but **abandons the z=2 UV completion** that is the
  point of A3. This is a fundamental reformulation of CCEF, outside its current axioms.

## Honest synthesis (end of this line of inquiry)
- The boost-Goldstone photon — the one mechanism that could have given an exactly
  relativistic photon while keeping matter's A3 — **cannot be realized in CCEF**, because
  A3 explicitly breaks Lorentz boosts. The would-be protector is killed by the field it was
  meant to protect against.
- This closes the loop opened over the last steps:
  baryon-light ⇒ A3 large ⇒ photon (n-construct) inherits A3 ⇒ catastrophic LV ⇒ need A3
  tiny ⇒ baryon too heavy. No internal CCEF mechanism breaks the cycle.
- The tension is now traced to a single root: **A3 is simultaneously (a) CCEF's UV
  completion/regulator, (b) the universal source of Lorentz violation, and (c) (via the
  matched B2) the controller of the baryon mass.** One term wears three hats with
  conflicting requirements.

## What this means for CCEF (constructive)
- **The gravity/cosmology spine stands** — it depends on A3 only logarithmically, is fine
  across the whole A3 range, and its universality (c_gw=c_light) is a genuine virtue. None
  of the tension touches it.
- **The particle/EM sector needs a structural change**, not a tweak: a **Lorentz-invariant
  UV completion** (e.g. (□n)²-type, or a covariant regulator) that (a) regulates the UV,
  (b) does not source observable LV, and (c) still yields the topological soliton spectrum.
  Whether such a completion can keep the baryon light is the real open question — and it is
  a reformulation of the UV sector, not a within-axioms computation.
- Equivalently: CCEF must decide whether z=2 Lifshitz is fundamental (then LV is generic and
  the photon/baryon tension is real) or merely an effective regulator standing in for a
  deeper Lorentz-invariant UV theory.

## Caveats
- The obstruction (boosts explicitly broken ⇒ no protected Goldstone) is a symmetry
  statement and is robust given CCEF's z=2 kinetic structure.
- Option (i)'s "~8×" uses the soliton-matched B2 and the converged E·R(B2) table.
- Option (ii) is flagged as a direction, not endorsed; whether (□n)² preserves CCEF's other
  successes (graviton, cosmology, solitons) was not checked here.
