   the flux through each triangle of grains (edge phases that are pure gauge can be removed by rephasing grains)
   real couplings (grain Hamiltonian v1)          triangle fluxes [0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0]  -> physical T (and CP) conserved
   pure-gauge phases (phi_ij = a_i - a_j)         triangle fluxes [0.0, 0.0, 0.0, 0.0]  -> physical T (and CP) conserved
   phases with net flux through triangles         triangle fluxes [0.4, 0.2, 0.0, 0.2]  -> physical T (and CP) VIOLATED
   real Hamiltonian: C = True , P = True , T = True  -> v1 conserves C, P, T separately

   note: the 4 faces of a junction form a closed surface, so edge-phase fluxes through them sum to zero:
   edge phases can bias a single face's chirality but NOT the junction's total chirality CHI
